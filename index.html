This Privacy Policy describes how Triple Tile: Cozy Quest ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you download, install, and play our game. We are dedicated to respecting your privacy and offering a transparent, secure environment.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. On-Device Storage (Offline Game Data):
Your gameplay state (such as levels unlocked, high score, star rankings, coin balance, and sound/visual preferences) is stored solely on your local device via HTML5 LocalStorage. None of this gameplay data is transmitted to or saved on any remote server.

B. Third-Party Advertising Services (Google AdMob):
To provide this application free of charge, we incorporate Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical identifiers and diagnostics from your device:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Android Advertising ID (AAID) and device hardware details.
  • Location & Network Data: IP address (coarse country/regional geographic location) and internet service details.
  • Ad Interactions & Performance: Impressions, click events, rewarded video completion, and crash logs.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Monetization is powered by Google AdMob and Google Play Services. To review Google's privacy practices, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use their services
  • Google Advertising Policies

4. Purpose of Data Processing

Information processed by Google AdMob is strictly utilized to:

  • Deliver non-intrusive Banner, natural Interstitial, and opt-in Rewarded Video ads.
  • Enforce frequency capping (preventing the repeated display of identical ads).
  • Detect fraud, invalid impressions, and security anomalies.
  • Diagnose app crashes and maintain compatibility across Android devices.

5. How to Opt-Out & Control Your Advertising ID

You can manage, reset, or completely delete your Android Advertising Identifier (AAID) at any time:

  • On Android: Open Settings > Google > Ads > select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • You can also visit Google Ad Settings to manage ad preferences.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Triple Tile: Cozy Quest is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal information of a child under 13 was inadvertently processed, please contact us immediately for deletion.

7. Data Deletion & Retention

You can delete all locally saved game progress at any time by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Triple Tile: Cozy Quest > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Cache.
